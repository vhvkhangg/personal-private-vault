## Accepted functionality — bounded S2A delivery

1. Authenticated **manual** `POST /api/v1/feed/sources/{sourceId}/refresh-rss` for enabled `RSS` source with a configured `feedUrl`. 2. Authenticated **cached** `GET /api/v1/feed/radar/combined`: rolling 24-hour unified HN + RSS preview, source attributed and bounded; no outbound I/O on a read.
