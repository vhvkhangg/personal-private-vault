#### B04/B05 HTTP contract groups

Inventory every in-scope method/path and material profile or flag condition separately. Assign a stable contract-group ID in the HTTP matrix using the same four attributes for every row:

1. **Capability:** the owned resource or workflow defined by the published contract and owning service. Distinct resources or workflows with separate domain invariants form separate groups, including when one module or controller handles both.
2. **Operation:** the same kind of behavior, such as detail, paged list/search, create, update, domain transition, binary transfer or framework/auth operation. Split operations when validation, side effects, status/error semantics, DTO or field exposure, paging or cache guarantees differ for a required assertion.
3. **Access:** the same authorization predicate and enforcement point, principal/profile and material feature-flag state. Split an authorization or exposure variant when matcher, filter, method security, resource eligibility or ownership checks, provider gate or profile-specific projection differ.
4. **Evidence path:** the same outcome-relevant handler/service/query/serializer mechanism for the assertion. Distinct mechanisms require their own witness unless the matrix records concrete source equivalence for the assertion. Document the common mechanism or equivalent paths and cite the applicable baseline lines.

For each group, require at least one dispatched allowed-case witness for the applicable B04 behavior and an executed B05 denial witness for every applicable distinct denial boundary in that group. A group may need more than one assertion to cover its applicable response and privacy guarantees. Mark a positive or denial condition `N/A` only with an explicit contract and matcher/source reason, such as a deny-only fallback or a public endpoint with no profile-based denial. This applies per condition and preserves any independent input, credential and safe-error checks required by the contract. The generated OpenAPI inventory and static method/path mapping still reconcile all applicable individual routes under their relevant configuration.

For each representative assertion, cite the exercised method/path, principal/flags, actual assertion and cohort. List the other credited rows and explain why the outcome-relevant mechanisms and conditions match. Credit applies to the assertion demonstrated. A shared pre-controller denial may be reused across groups only for that denial if the matcher/filter predicate, position, status and no-dispatch behavior are demonstrably the same for every credited row. It cannot cover their allowed behavior, handler-dependent eligibility, field exposure, paging or cache effects. Split coverage where equivalence cannot be shown. This requires qualified representative evidence per group and boundary, with no blanket requirement to execute every route.

**Knowledge example:** Study, Information, Vocabulary and Notes are separate capabilities although they share `KnowledgeController`. A Vocabulary detail or review cohort cannot cover the dispatched allowed behavior or resource-dependent privacy of Study, Information or Notes. A common pre-controller denial can carry only the narrow denial credit described above. If those groups lack their required witnesses after cohort qualification, report the exact uncovered group and assertion as `NOT_VERIFIED` and return `BLOCKED_B0` unless an explicit criterion-specific Owner decision covers the gap. G5 remains unmet with any unresolved mandatory gap.

Qualify reused execution per cohort and claimed assertion. Record the actual run, methods or parameterized invocations, exit/result and receipt or independently corroborated published artifact. Compare relevant source/test blobs, migrations, dependencies, effective flags, environment and fixture between its SHA and the audit SHA, including differences that can affect the outcome. Credit only preserved behavior covered by the actual assertion and conditions. A transitively unchanged path, static test declaration or unqualified aggregate does not establish evidence. Retain unavailable receipts, material changes and coverage gaps as `NOT_VERIFIED`. Count fresh and reused invocations once.

Optional or out-of-scope gaps may remain explicit, including representative latency or query-plan measurements for B17, secondary interleavings and measurements beyond the scoped fixture, live providers, real Owner data, production activation and disaster recovery. An in-scope mandatory gap can become an accepted limitation only through an explicit Owner decision naming the criterion, missing evidence and exact boundary, followed by independent verification of its applicability.
