## General gate and phase semantics

- **One numbered phase = one independently prepared/reviewed/implemented/closed scope.** Each phase 17–22 gets its *own* P-N `README.md`, `preparation-review.md`, Codex pre-handoff acceptance, owner commit, Codex handoff, Antigravity implementation/tests, Codex final review, and owner push. Only **Phase 17** preparation exists now; later phases listed here are planned scopes, **not prepared/approved implementation handoffs**.
- Exception: P-17 starts from completed Phase 15 (`BACKEND_AUDIT_READY`), even though Phase 16 is deferred. P-18 requires frozen Phase 17; P-19 requires frozen 18; and so on. Phase 16 needs new owner authorization after Phase 22, not a false "previous-number" dependency on Phase 15/17.
- **Security is not deferred** to a "hardening phase": every slice must independently satisfy privacy, consent, non-private field allowlists, deterministic source-commit fencing, offline/egress denial, revocation/lifecycle and appropriate per-run outcome reporting for any capability it exposes. No security-vulnerable "MVP" can be accepted temporarily.
- Scope extensions beyond the initial approved Fiction/Film and explicit external files require **individual source/field allowlist review** and owner's authorization; do not silently allow whole-module dumps, Finance/Personal/Journal/Auth or unknown content. Ideaverse remains out of scope until the owner later integrates it in read-only mode.
- Do not freeze model/embedding/OCR/chunking numbers without benchmark evidence. Design for Windows 11 (i5-9600K, 16 GB RAM, RTX 2070 8 GB dedicated VRAM), under 500 **files**, not unlimited bytes/pages/chunks.
